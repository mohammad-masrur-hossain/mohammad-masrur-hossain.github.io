About

At Intel I develop and optimize wafer-level metrology recipes — Brightfield and Darkfield optical inspection — across EMIB-T and Hybrid Bonding packaging architectures. I use SPC, DOE and FMEA to judge tool capability, run root cause analysis on excursions, and work with OEM suppliers to keep tools available in 24/7 production.

Before packaging, my M.S. research at the University of Washington built a high-throughput synthesis platform for metal-organic frameworks, characterized with XRD, SEM, TEM and TGA. Add simulation work in ANSYS, SOLIDWORKS and EBSILON®, and the result is a process-and-materials skill set that carries beyond semiconductors into manufacturing and energy systems.
